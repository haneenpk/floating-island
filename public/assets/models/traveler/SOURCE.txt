  node scripts/unface-model.mjs Rogue_Hooded.glb faceless.glb Rogue_Head_Hooded
  node scripts/trim-glb-animations.mjs faceless.glb traveler.glb \
      Idle Walking_A Running_A Jump_Full_Long Jump_Idle

Changes made to the original:
  - The face is removed from the mesh, not hidden. Every triangle on the head
    was sampled against the character sheet and the ones that came back skin,
    brow or eye were dropped - 445 of them, leaving 328 of hood cloth. There is
    no lighting or camera angle that can reveal a feature that is not there.
  - 71 of its 76 animation clips removed, keeping Idle, Walking_A, Running_A,
    Jump_Full_Long and Jump_Idle; unreferenced accessors and buffer views
    pruned. 3.43 MB -> 0.52 MB.

Changes made at load time (src/scene/character/Traveler.ts):
  - The costume is repainted off the character sheet, which is an 8 x 4 grid of
    flat gradients with one cell per surface: the pack's green becomes the blue
    of the hood and cloak, the tunic and sleeves become cream, the trousers
    charcoal, the fittings brass. See travelerPalette.ts.
  - The hood is filled with an unlit black form, since removing the face leaves
    the hood an open shell.
  - The pack's weapon attachments (knives, crossbows, throwable) are hidden.
  - Backpack, bedroll, belt satchel, walking staff and hanging lantern are
    built from primitives rather than downloaded - no extra assets, and they
    match the reference more closely than any stock prop would.
